    Why DocentAI

    Not another general-purpose chatbot.

    General-purpose AI assistants learn from the internet and nobody in the classroom supervises them. DocentAI puts the teacher at the centre.

    General chatbot DocentAI
    Source General chatbotThe internet, with no way to tell where each fact comes from The material the teacher uploaded and validated, cited in every answer
    When it doesn't know General chatbotIt may make up a convincing answer It says so and points the student to the teacher
    Help General chatbotUsually hands over the full solution Hints or the solution, as the teacher decides
    Mistakes General chatbotEvery conversation starts from scratch Remembers recurring mistakes and offers practice
    Teacher General chatbotLeft out Sets up the tutor, sees the class and approves the quizzes

    For students

    Hints before solutions

    The teacher chooses how the tutor helps. Change the option and watch the answer.

    • Direct: the full steps
    • Hints first: the solution, if asked for
    • Hints only: the student takes the last step
    I can't solve this: 2(x − 3) = 10

    For students

    Repeated mistakes get worked on

    The tutor names the specific mistake. If it comes back, it offers practice until it's overcome.

    • Exercises typed or as a photo
    • Feedback on the mistake, not just a mark
    • Quizzes that adapt to each student
